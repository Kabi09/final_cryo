Workflow 12 — FINANCE & ACCOUNTS
Now we move into the Finance/Accounts workflow.

This module does not replace the Sales, Procurement, or Service workflows. It receives financial transactions from those modules and tracks money, invoices, receivables, payables, adjustments, refunds, and accounting synchronization.

The source specifically includes Final Invoice, customer receivables, vendor payables, adjustments, credit/debit notes, refunds, and vendor payment control through PO + GRN + Vendor Invoice (3-way match).
